Fort Kit · concept · industry system

A 3D sketchpad for things you build in the backyard

Three directions for the same product: freeform drag-and-snap 3D modelling on a phone, with wood, brick, metal, rope and climbing holds, ending in a cut list, a cost and a safety read. All three phones below run real geometry — drag inside any screen to orbit it.

Assumed user
A parent or hobbyist builder sketching an idea from their head, plus kids riffing on it.
Assumed unit
One foot. Parts snap to a 1 ft grid and levels step in whole feet, so the cut list stays buildable.
Assumed grammar
Materials read as tonal value, not hue — the system is mono steel, so brick, metal and rope differ by weight.
